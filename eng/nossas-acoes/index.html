02 / Children, adolescents and families

Virtues for every stage of life.

The Guia das Virtudes collection connects Virtology with situations that children, adolescents and families experience at home, at school and in their communities. Stories, affirmations, play, exercises and journals help them understand each virtue and put it into practice in everyday relationships.

The approach combines reading and experience: recognising a situation, choosing a concrete action, practising it and reflecting on what happened. Educators and family members contribute through guidance, example and continued involvement.

Explore the records in our presentation

An approach for each educational stage.

Ages 3–6 · Book 1

Early Childhood Education

Eight virtues explored through spoken stories, play and drawing. The adult’s presence, relationship and example guide the children’s experience.

Ages 6–10 · Book 2

Early Primary Years

Sixteen virtues, with meanings, affirmations, habits and stories that lead to choosing a concrete action, practising it and recording the experience, supported by an educator.

Ages 11–14 · Book 3

Later School Years

Eleven virtues, with stories, discussions, habits and a journal. Students reflect on identity, relationships, the consequences of their choices and commitments they can make.

Foundations and guidance

Teacher’s Book

Seven conditions and thirteen steps support planning, activities, group discussions, records and family involvement, with attention to the age of each class.

Explore the books at Instituto Virtudes

From understanding to everyday practice.

  1. Understand

    Connect the virtue with a story or a real situation.

  2. Choose

    Identify a concrete action to try in everyday life.

  3. Practise

    Work with guidance from an educator and family involvement.

  4. Reflect

    Record, share and revisit what happened.

The reference schedule organises understanding and practice over two initial weeks, followed by further reviews. Revisiting the material at 7, 30, 90 and 180 days maintains continuity. Each institution plans its sessions and monitors the records through its own responsible team.

Continuing into high school

Quem manda em mim?

Choices, conflicts, peer pressure and violence prevention are part of the work with adolescents. The Student’s Book offers reflections and exercises that connect these topics with young people’s own experiences.

The Teacher’s Book guides study cycles, group discussions, practice and records, connecting school, staff and families. Together, the two books support educators’ facilitation and learning to build respectful relationships.

Experience and institutional research

From practical experience to a study with families.

Video: Virtology — Instituto Virtudes · Nova Geração Brasil

Watch on YouTubeWatch Raquel Mendonça’s testimonials

At the Nova Geração Brasil NGO in São João del-Rei, the experience with Guia das Virtudes was also documented in an institutional study with adult family members of the children and adolescents served. In January 2026, five weekly sessions explored Humility, Gentleness, Patience and Responsibility through activities and everyday practice.

The study by Eduardo Casarotto, compiled by Instituto Virtudes in May 2026, brings together before-and-after assessments, the facilitators’ report and participant testimonials. Six family members took part in the intervention; five completed both assessments. Four of these five increased their combined scores for the four virtues addressed.

Article and recognition

Virtology and child and adolescent mental health.

The paper Shaping the future: Virtology as an ethical path to child and adolescent mental health received first place at the VI Brazilian Congress of Health, held online on 11 June 2025. The certificate names Eduardo Casarotto as the author.

The article by Eduardo Casarotto and Flávia Fontana was published in Educere — Revista da Educação da UNIPAR on 24 April 2026. It discusses virtue development, ethical education and the mental health of children and adolescents.

03 / Frente de atuação

Saúde mental,
dependências e compulsões

O Novo Eu para pessoas com dependências e compulsões utiliza o livro O Que Nos Prende. O percurso contempla dependências relacionadas a substâncias e a comportamentos como apostas, telas, alimentação, compras e outras compulsões, com conteúdos próprios para a pessoa e a família.

A proposta pode ser construída com serviços de saúde, instituições e redes de apoio. A aproximação com a rede pública de saúde é uma frente de cooperação que o Humanar busca desenvolver.

Uma experiência na Clínica da Alma

Na Clínica da Alma, em Mato Grosso, aulas e prática de virtudes integram a trajetória do programa em comunidade terapêutica. Os relatos dos participantes mencionam perdão, perseverança, brandura, aceitação e fé.
